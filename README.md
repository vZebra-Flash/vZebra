# vZebra

Professional Video Color Pipeline for Short Filmmakers

## What It Does

vZebra applies a precise, mathematical ACES-based cinematic finish to your 
finished short film — in one click.

- No LUTs
- No presets
- No creative filters
- Just your vision, preserved

## Features

- ACES-based Narkowicz tone mapping
- Works with MP4, MKV, MOV, AVI, WebM
- Output: MP4 (MPEG-4 Part 2 video, MP3 audio)
- 7-day free trial
- $39 one-time lifetime license

## System Requirements

- Windows 10 or later (64-bit)
- 4 GB RAM minimum, 8 GB recommended
